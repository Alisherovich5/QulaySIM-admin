The sourcing model, for whoever needs it: both wholesalers are read from their APIs; every supplier's price for every tariff is kept, not just the winner. The cheapest available becomes the tariff's cost and the route a paid order takes, and the dearer stays as the fallback for a stock-out or an API outage. Customers see one tariff at one price.
